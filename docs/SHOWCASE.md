# Showcase shot list

A plan for recording the project honestly in about 90 seconds. Every shot is
something the app actually does; nothing needs to be staged.

## Before recording

- Record from the **portable build**, not a debug build. It is what people
  download.
- Unzip it fresh into an empty folder so the first-launch state is real: blank
  canvas, no clips, no project restored. If a project reopens, that is the
  restore feature working — close the window and clear
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\CreativeCraft\projects` for the true first run.
- 1920×1080 or larger. The window is dense; full screen helps.
- Have one short piece of media ready (a clip, a photo). Ten seconds of footage is
  enough for every shot below.

## Shots, in order

| # | What you do | What it shows | Say something like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Launch the app | Blank Photo canvas, tab bar at the bottom | "It opens empty. No demo, no sample project." |
| 2 | Point at the bottom tab bar, don't click yet | Seven tabs, DaVinci-style | "Edit, Effects, Photo, Audio, 3D, Render, AI — one window." |
| 3 | Click Edit | Empty project, 0 items, no sequences | "A real video editor, not a mock-up." |
| 4 | Import the clip, put it on the timeline | A cut exists | "FilmCraft, doing what it does." |
| 5 | Click Effects | EffectCraft is already open, still running | "Effects stays loaded. Nothing restarts." |
| 6 | Send a frame to Photo (Edit → Send frame to Photo) | The frame arrives as a layer | "The editor and the design talk to each other." |
| 7 | In Photo, switch Vector → Pixel → Layout | One document, three personas | "One design, three ways in — the Affinity idea." |
| 8 | Place the design on the timeline | Clips appear on the sequence | "A design becomes a clip." |
| 9 | Click Audio | The same cut's audio, not a re-import | "Same project, different room." |
| 10 | Click 3D, hold the shot for two seconds | The placeholder panel | "3D isn't real yet. I'm not going to pretend." |
| 11 | Click AI | Maestro opens in its own view | "A local AI studio, running on this machine." |
| 12 | Close on the tab bar | The whole window | "One download, no installer. The source is on GitHub." |

## Claims to avoid

- Don't say "professional" about 0.1.0. Say "early", then show the things that do
  work.
- Don't show Red Giant, Boris FX or Topaz. They load but do not render; showing
  them invites the question you can't answer on camera.
- Don't call the 3D tab "coming soon" with a date. It is a placeholder.
- Don't say "open source" about the whole build: your shell is MIT, the bundled
  apps keep their own licences, and the trademarks stay out.

## One extra shot worth having

The GitHub page itself, scrolled from the description down through the two
first-launch screenshots to the Releases entry. Ten seconds. It tells viewers
where the download is, which is the only action you actually want from them.
